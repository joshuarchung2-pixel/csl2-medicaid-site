My Medi-Cal sign-up checklist

Check things off as you go. You don't need every paper to start, so apply anyway. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

1Gather your papers

  • Photo ID or driver's license
  • Social Security card, if you have one
  • Immigration papers, if you have them
  • Latest pay stub
  • Last tax return (Schedule C if self-employed)
  • Benefit letters, like unemployment or disability
  • Receipts for child care or child support you pay

Paid in cash? You may tell the county your income yourself. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

2Apply one way

  • Online at BenefitsCal.com or CoveredCA.com
  • By phone with your county office (numbers below)
  • In person at your county office
  • By mail with a paper application in your language

You can apply any time of year. (DHCS, n.d.-a, n.d.-e)

3After you apply

  • Open every letter and text from the county
  • Send any extra proof they ask for, fast
  • Find a Medi-Cal dentist near you (Smile, California finder)
  • Send back renewal forms on time

(DHCS, n.d.-d; Smile, California, n.d.-a)

4Children's dental visits

  • First visit by the first tooth or first birthday
  • Checkup and cleaning every 6 months, covered by Medi-Cal
  • Ask about sealants for back teeth

Babies up to 3 can get exams every 3 months. (Smile, California, n.d.-a, n.d.-c)
