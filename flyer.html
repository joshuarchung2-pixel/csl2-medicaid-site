Smiles Start with Medi-Cal

For California families

No insurance? Your kids may still be covered.

Kids can get dental care through Medi-Cal.

Medi-Cal is California's free or low-cost health plan, and it includes dental care like checkups and fillings. (DHCS, n.d.-c)

  • Kids under 19 qualify no matter their immigration status. (DHCS, 2026a)
  • In 2026 a family of 4 can earn about $87,780 a year and the kids can still get Medi-Cal. (DHCS, 2025; ASPE, 2026)
  • You can apply any time of year. (DHCS, n.d.-e)
