For California families without health insurance

Free or low-cost dental care for your kids starts with Medi-Cal.

Medi-Cal is California's free or low-cost health coverage. It includes dental care: checkups, cleanings, fillings, and more. Many working families qualify. This guide shows you how to sign up, step by step.

Check if you may qualify How to apply
  • Kids under 19 can get Medi-Cal no matter their immigration status. (DHCS, 2026a)
  • Dental care is included for kids, teens, pregnant people, and adults. (DHCS, n.d.-c)
  • Apply any time of year online, by phone, by mail, or in person. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

Why this matters

Health is shaped by more than brushing and flossing. Experts call the conditions where we live, work, and grow up social determinants of health. (ODPHP, n.d.) This project focuses on two of the five Healthy People 2030 areas:

  • Economic Stability
  • Health Care Access and Quality

2×

Money changes smiles

Children ages 6 to 9 from lower-income homes are more than twice as likely to have untreated cavities (25%) as children from higher-income homes (10%). (CDC, 2024)

1 in 2

Cavities start early

By age 9, half of children have had a cavity in their baby or adult teeth. Many kids get cavities as early as age 2. (CDC, 2024; Smile, California, n.d.-a)

34M

Lost school hours

About 34 million school hours are lost each year in the U.S. because of unplanned (emergency) dental care. Tooth pain can make it hard to eat, speak, and learn. (CDC, 2024)

What gets in the way, and how Medi-Cal helps

  • Cost: Medi-Cal gives free or low-cost coverage to people who qualify, including dental exams, cleanings, and fillings. (DHCS, n.d.-c)
  • Transportation: Medi-Cal covers rides to Medi-Cal appointments for people who need them. (DHCS, n.d.-c)
  • Language: The paper Medi-Cal application comes in 12 languages, and Covered California has phone help and enrollers in many languages. (DHCS, n.d.-a; Covered California, n.d.)
  • Paperwork: If you get paid in cash or have no proof of income, you may be able to tell the county your income yourself ("self-attest"). (DHCS, n.d.-e)

What Medi-Cal dental covers

Medi-Cal Dental gives free or low-cost services. Here's what the program lists for each age group. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

Babies (0–3)

  • Dental exams every 3 months
  • Cleanings and fluoride varnish every 6 months
  • X-rays, fillings, tooth removal, emergency care

First visit: when the first tooth comes in or by the first birthday, whichever comes first. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

Kids (4–12)

  • Exams, cleanings, and fluoride varnish every 6 months
  • Sealants that protect back teeth from cavities
  • X-rays, fillings, root canals, tooth removal, emergency care

(Smile, California, n.d.-c)

Teens (13–20)

  • Everything for kids, plus crowns and deep cleanings
  • Braces for teens who qualify
  • Partial and full dentures if needed

(Smile, California, n.d.-d)

Adults and pregnancy

  • Exam and cleaning every 12 months
  • Fillings, crowns, root canals, deep cleanings
  • Partial and full dentures, tooth removal, emergency care

(Smile, California, n.d.-b)

Coming change for some adults: Starting July 1, 2027, some adult members (because of their immigration status) will get only emergency dental care, such as treatment for bad tooth pain, infections, and tooth removal. Children 0–18 and pregnant people keep full dental benefits. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

How to apply, step by step

You can apply any time of year. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

  1. Gather your papers

    Use the checklist below. If you're missing something, apply anyway. Your county office can help. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

  2. Pick the easiest way to apply

    • Online: BenefitsCal.com or CoveredCA.com
    • Phone: call your county office (numbers below)
    • Mail: download a paper application in your language
    • In person: visit a county office

    (DHCS, n.d.-a)

  3. Watch your mail, texts, and email

    The county may ask for more proof. Open letters right away and answer quickly so your application doesn't stall. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

  4. Find a Medi-Cal dentist

    Search the Smile, California dentist finder or call Medi-Cal Dental at 1-800-322-6384 (free call, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.). (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

  5. Renew on time to keep your coverage

    Keep your contact information up to date and send back renewal packets. Starting March 1, 2027, some adults 19 to 64 will renew every six months instead of once a year. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

What to bring

These are examples of what the county may ask for. You don't need everything to start. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

Who you are

  • Driver's license or photo ID
  • Social Security card, if you have one
  • Immigration papers, if you have them

What you earn

  • Most recent pay stub
  • Last tax return (Form 1040; Schedule C if self-employed)
  • Benefit letters (unemployment, disability, Social Security)

Where you live and what you pay

  • Your home or mailing address (no proof needed)
  • Receipts for child care or child support you pay
  • Paid in cash? You may tell the county yourself.

Myths vs. facts

Tap each myth to see the facts.

Myth "My kids can't get Medi-Cal because of our immigration status."
Fact

Children under 19 can apply for full Medi-Cal, with dental, no matter their immigration status. So can pregnant people, during pregnancy and for one year after. (DHCS, 2026a)

Myth "We both work, so we make too much."
Fact

Kids qualify at higher incomes than adults: up to 266% of the federal poverty level. In 2026 that's about $87,780 a year for a family of four. (DHCS, 2025; ASPE, 2026)

Myth "Medi-Cal doesn't cover the dentist."
Fact

Dental is part of Medi-Cal: exams, X-rays, cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canals, and more. (DHCS, n.d.-c)

Myth "I have to wait for open enrollment."
Fact

You can apply for Medi-Cal at any time of the year. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

Myth "Baby teeth fall out, so cavities in them don't matter."
Fact

Baby teeth help kids chew and speak. Losing them too early from decay can cause problems with adult teeth, like growing in crooked. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

Myth "Medi-Cal is ending for everyone because of the new rules."
Fact

Most Medi-Cal members will not see any changes to their coverage. Children keep full Medi-Cal and full dental. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

Myth "We have savings, so Medi-Cal will say no."
Fact

The asset limit that returned January 1, 2026, applies mainly to adults 65 and older and people with disabilities, not to children. (DHCS, n.d.-d; HCA, n.d.)

What's changing in Medi-Cal (2026–2028)

State budget decisions and federal law are changing some Medi-Cal rules. Here's the official timeline, in plain words. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

StartsWhat changesWho it affects
Jan 1, 2026 NowEnrollment freeze: some adults can no longer newly sign up for full Medi-Cal. People already enrolled keep it if they renew on time. Jan 1, 2026 NowAsset limit returns: $130,000 for one person, plus $65,000 for each extra person (up to 10).Adults 65+, people with disabilities, nursing-home residents, and some others.
Jan 1, 2027Work rules: work, volunteer, go to school, or train at least 80 hours a month (or earn at least $580 a month).Some adults 19–64. Not children, pregnant people, or parents and caregivers of children 0–13.
