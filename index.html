For California families without health insurance

Your kids can get dental care through Medi-Cal.

Medi-Cal is California's free or low-cost health plan, and it comes with dental care. Many working families qualify. Answer 3 quick questions to see your next step.

Can you get Medi-Cal?

Kids under 19 can likely get free Medi-Cal if your family earns about a year or less, before taxes. Up to about , kids may get low-cost coverage for a small monthly fee. Their immigration status does not matter. (DHCS, 2025; ASPE, 2026; DHCS, 2026a)

If you're pregnant, you can get full Medi-Cal with dental, no matter your immigration status. It lasts the whole pregnancy and 1 year after. Pregnancy has its own income rules, so apply even if you think you earn too much. (DHCS, 2026a)

Adults 19 to 64 can qualify if the family earns a year or less. Since January 1, 2026, most adults without a qualifying immigration status can't newly sign up for full Medi-Cal, but they can still get emergency and pregnancy care. (DHCS, n.d.-b, n.d.-d)

Adults 65 and older have different rules. Since January 1, 2026, Medi-Cal counts savings and other assets for this group (up to $130,000 for one person, plus $65,000 for each extra person). Call your county office to talk it through. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

Dental care you can get

  • Baby: exams every 3 months, plus cleanings and fluoride every 6 months. First visit by the first tooth or first birthday. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)
  • Child: checkups and cleanings every 6 months, sealants on back teeth, fillings and root canals. (Smile, California, n.d.-c)
  • Teen: checkups every 6 months, sealants and crowns. Braces for teens who qualify. (Smile, California, n.d.-d)
  • Pregnant: full dental care, and you may have no yearly dollar limit. (Smile, California, n.d.-b)
  • Adult: a checkup and cleaning every year, fillings, crowns and dentures, up to $1,800 a year. (Smile, California, n.d.-b)
  • 65 and older: Medi-Cal Dental covers older adults too. Call 1-800-322-6384 to ask what's covered for you. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

Heard any of these?

Tap a card to flip it.

  • Myth

    “My kids can't get Medi-Cal because of our immigration status.”

    Fact

    Kids under 19 can get full Medi-Cal with dental no matter their immigration status. So can pregnant people, during pregnancy and for 1 year after. (DHCS, 2026a)

  • Myth

    “We both work, so we make too much.”

    Fact

    Kids qualify at higher incomes than adults. In 2026 a family of 4 can earn about $87,780 a year and the kids can still get Medi-Cal. (DHCS, 2025; ASPE, 2026)

  • Myth

    “Medi-Cal doesn't cover the dentist.”

    Fact

    Dental is part of Medi-Cal. That includes exams, X-rays, cleanings, fillings, crowns and root canals. (DHCS, n.d.-c)

  • Myth

    “I have to wait for open enrollment.”

    Fact

    You can apply for Medi-Cal any time of year. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

  • Myth

    “Baby teeth fall out, so cavities in them don't matter.”

    Fact

    Baby teeth help kids chew and speak. Losing them too early to decay can make adult teeth grow in crooked. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

  • Myth

    “Medi-Cal is ending because of the new rules.”

    Fact

    Most Medi-Cal members won't see any change. Kids keep full Medi-Cal and full dental. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

How to apply

You can apply any time of year. If you're missing a paper, apply anyway. Your county office can help. (DHCS, n.d.-e)

Print the checklist

  1. Pick a way to apply

    Online at BenefitsCal.com or CoveredCA.com. You can also call or visit your county office (numbers below), or mail a paper application in your language. (DHCS, n.d.-a)

  2. Watch your mail and texts

    The county may ask for more proof. Answer fast so your application doesn't get stuck. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

  3. Find a Medi-Cal dentist

    Use the Smile, California dentist finder or call Medi-Cal Dental at 1-800-322-6384. The call is free, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Smile, California, n.d.-a)

  4. Renew on time

    Keep your address and phone up to date and send back renewal forms. Starting March 1, 2027, some adults 19 to 64 will renew every 6 months. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

What's changing in Medi-Cal

Kids under 19 and pregnant people keep full Medi-Cal and full dental. Most members won't see any change. Some rules for adults are changing between 2026 and 2028, so adults should renew on time. (DHCS, n.d.-d)

See the dates
  • Since Jan. 1, 2026: most adults 19 and older without a qualifying immigration status can't newly sign up for full Medi-Cal. People who have it keep it if they renew on time. If coverage ends, they have 3 months to sign up again.
  • Since Jan. 1, 2026: an asset limit is back for adults 65 and older and people with disabilities ($130,000 for one person, plus $65,000 for each extra person). It does not apply to kids. (HCA, n.d.)
  • Jan. 1, 2027: some adults 19 to 64 will need to work, study or volunteer 80 hours a month, or earn at least $580 a month. Parents of kids 13 and under don't have to.
  • July 1, 2027: some adults, based on immigration status, will get only emergency dental care and pay $30 a month to keep full Medi-Cal. (HCA, n.d.)
  • Oct. 1, 2028: small copays for some adults earning more than $15,560 a year. No copays for kids, checkups or community health centers.

See how each immigration status is affected on the DHCS immigration status chart. (DHCS, 2026a)
