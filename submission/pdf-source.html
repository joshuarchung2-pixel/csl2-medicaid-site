Smiles Start with Medi-CalEN / ES educational website

CSL II Dental Public Health Educational Project

Western University of Health Sciences, College of Dental Medicine · Modality: Educational website

NameJoshua Chung, DMD Class of 2029
TopicMedicaid Enrollment (Medi-Cal): Encouraging eligible, uninsured families to enroll
SDOH addressedEconomic Stability and Health Care Access and Quality (Healthy People 2030 domains). The site ties lack of insurance, income, and cost barriers to untreated decay in children (lower-income children ages 6–9 have 25% untreated caries vs. 10% in higher-income homes), and addresses language, transportation, paperwork, and immigration-related barriers to enrolling.
AudienceGeneral public: uninsured California families with children (Los Angeles and Inland Empire resources). Plain language, English and Spanish.
Website linkhttps://joshuarchung2-pixel.github.io/csl2-medicaid-site/
Site contents3-tap guided check (county, household size, who needs coverage) with a personal result card · tap-a-tooth coverage map with an age slider (no teeth yet, baby, mixed, adult, and no natural teeth) · myth flip cards · how to apply, plus one 2026 changes callout · local help (county offices, clinics, phone lines) · printable fridge checklist and QR clinic flyer (English and Spanish) · APA references
