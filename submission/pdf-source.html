CSL II Dental Public Health Educational Project

Modality: Educational website

Topic: Medicaid Enrollment (Medi-Cal): Encouraging eligible, uninsured families to enroll

SDOH addressed: Economic Stability and Health Care Access and Quality (Healthy People 2030 domains). The site ties lack of insurance, income, and cost barriers to untreated decay in children (lower-income children ages 6–9 have 25% untreated caries vs. 10% in higher-income homes), and addresses language, transportation, paperwork, and immigration-related barriers to enrolling.

Audience: General public: uninsured California families with children (Los Angeles and Inland Empire resources). Plain language, English and Spanish.

Website link: https://joshuarchung2-pixel.github.io/csl2-medicaid-site/

Site contents: A 3-tap guided check (county, household size, who needs coverage) with a personal result card; a tap-a-tooth coverage map with an age slider (no teeth yet, baby, mixed, adult, and no natural teeth); myth flip cards; how to apply, plus one 2026 changes callout; local help (county offices, clinics, phone lines); a printable fridge checklist and QR clinic flyer (English and Spanish); and APA references.
